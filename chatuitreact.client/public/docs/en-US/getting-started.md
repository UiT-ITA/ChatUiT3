ChatUiT is UiT's own chat service for AI models. You write, the assistant answers, and your conversations stay in your account.

## Sign in

Open ChatUiT and sign in with your UiT account. The login runs through Microsoft, the same as for email and Canvas. There is no separate password.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Your role at UiT decides which models and system assistants you see. If something described in this documentation is missing for you, it is most likely not enabled for your role.

## The screen

The app has three columns:

1. **Icon rail** on the far left. New chat, Chats, Assistants, Help, light/dark toggle, Settings and your profile menu.
2. **List column**. Shows what belongs to the section you are in: your chats, your assistants, the settings tabs or the help pages.
3. **Main area**. The conversation, or the page you opened.

On a phone the rail becomes a top bar and you switch between the list and the content with the back button.

## Your first message

1. Click **New chat** at the top of the rail.
2. Type a question in the box at the bottom and press `Enter`.
3. The answer streams in as it is written.

The chat gets a name from your first message and appears in the list under **Today**. You can rename it later.

## Pick an assistant

The button to the left of the message box shows which assistant you are talking to. An assistant is a ready-made setup: a model, a set of instructions and optionally some files. The default assistant is a good general choice. Read more under [Assistants](/help/assistants).
