Open **Settings** from the rail or from your profile menu.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Settings are stored in this browser, so they do not follow you to another computer.

## Appearance

![Replace with screenshot featuring the Appearance tab with theme and colour mode cards](placeholder)

- **Theme**: Blue, in UiT's colours, or Grey.
- **Colour mode**: Light, Dark, or Follow system. The sun and moon button in the rail switches between light and dark directly.
- **Text scale**: Small, Medium, Large or Extra large. Affects the whole app.
- **Language**: English or Norwegian. Changes the interface and this documentation.

## Personalization

- **Name**: what the welcome screen greets you with. Leave it empty to use the name from your UiT account.

> [!NOT_IMPLEMENTED]

The rest of the tab tells every assistant a little about you, so you do not have to repeat it in each chat:

- **Job role**, so answers fit your field and level.
- **Response style**: Creative, Balanced or Precise.
- **Response length**: Short, Medium or Long.
- **Custom instructions**: anything else the assistant should keep in mind, such as the language you want answers in.

## Data control

> [!NOT_IMPLEMENTED]

- **Chat retention period** and **Auto-delete old chats** remove chats older than the chosen period, from one week to never.
- **Export chat history** downloads all your chats in the chosen format: JSON, Markdown or plain text.
- **Delete all chats** removes every chat in your account. This cannot be undone.
