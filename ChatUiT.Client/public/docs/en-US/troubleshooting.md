## The answer is slow or never arrives

Long answers, high reasoning effort and tool calls all take time. The activity line under the answer shows what the model is doing. If nothing happens for a minute, press **Stop**, which puts your message back in the box, and send again. If the whole app is unresponsive, reload the page.

## "The conversation no longer fits in the model's context window"

The chat has grown past what the model can read at once. Start a new chat with a short summary of what matters, or remove attachments from the conversation. See [Managing chats](/help/chats#when-the-conversation-no-longer-fits).

## "File type not supported"

Only the types listed under [Working with files](/help/files#supported-file-types) can be attached. Convert the file, for example from an old .doc to .docx, or copy the text into the message.

## The assistant ignores my image

The assistant's model has no vision. Pick an assistant with the eye icon in the picker, or change the model on your own assistant.

## Tools are not available on my assistant

The model must support tools, and the tools must be ticked on the assistant. Both are set on the Assistants page.

## "Chat not found"

The chat was deleted, or the link belongs to another user's chat. Start a new chat from the rail.

## Something went wrong while starting the app

Click **Retry**. If that does not help, click **Sign out** and sign in again. A browser extension that blocks scripts or cookies can also prevent the login from completing.
